      Function, pathways and diseases load live from UniProt, Reactome and Open Targets, so they need an internet connection. The map itself is stored in this file.

      Disease lens

      Search any disease or trait. The map is coloured by how strongly each protein is linked to it (Open Targets association score), or by the most advanced clinical stage of drugs aimed at it.

        Map your own data

        Paste one gene per line as SYMBOL value (fold-change, score, count…), or load a CSV/TSV file. Negative and positive values get a diverging colour scale; all-positive values get a sequential one. A list of symbols alone is highlighted as present/absent. Nothing leaves your computer.

        Path finder

        Finds the chain of interactions that most strongly connects two proteins (highest combined STRING confidence, fewest hops).

        Guided tours

        Short, step-by-step walks through the map. Each step moves the camera for you.

        Tissue map

        Colour the whole map by expression in any tissue. Download rna_tissue_consensus.tsv from the Human Protein Atlas (proteinatlas.org/about/download), unzip it, and load it here. It is read in your browser and never uploaded.

        About Stellome

        • STRING: protein–protein interaction scores. CC BY 4.0.
        • Reactome: pathways used for the constellations. CC BY 4.0.
        • UniProtKB (function) and Open Targets (diseases, CC0), loaded live.

        How to cite. Please cite the underlying resources (check the current versions on their sites):

        • Szklarczyk D. et al. The STRING database in 2023. Nucleic Acids Res. 51, D638–D646 (2023).
        • Milacic M. et al. The Reactome Pathway Knowledgebase 2024. Nucleic Acids Res. 52, D672–D678 (2024).
        • The UniProt Consortium. UniProt: the Universal Protein Knowledgebase in 2023. Nucleic Acids Res. 51, D523–D531 (2023).
        • Ochoa D. et al. The next-generation Open Targets Platform. Nucleic Acids Res. 51, D1353–D1359 (2023).

        Each star is a protein; its size grows with its number of links. A constellation groups proteins by the Reactome top-level pathway they share with most of their partners. For research and education only.

        Highlight your gene list

        Paste official gene symbols (HGNC), separated by spaces, commas or new lines. Matches light up on the map and everything else fades.
